- Zero-parameter rule *extinct iff R0 < 1*: **83%** of 42 brains classified correctly.

## 2. Selection resolution

Saving of coasting over jittering: D = 0.00078 reward per predator-step.

| window T | noise sd | SNR | elites from credited half: measured | Gaussian model | share of selection on the leak |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 128 | 0.00327 | 0.24 | 71% (±4) | 60% | 5% |
| 512 | 0.00133 | 0.59 | 92% (±4) | 74% | 26% |
| 1024 | 0.00084 | 0.93 | 90% (±7) | 85% | 47% |

- Noise falls as T^-0.66 (independent steps: T^-0.50), so SNR = 1 at T ≈ 1145 steps (2249 if steps were independent). GA, ES and NEAT use T = 128.
- The Gaussian model under-predicts the measured elite shares, plausibly because the noise is skewed: the -1 death penalty, which spreads the bottom of the ranking more than the top where elites are chosen.
